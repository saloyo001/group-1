Welcome to Kijiko Palace

Kijiko Palace is the restaurant for anyone who wants great food any time of day or night. We are open 24/7 and ready to welcome you with warm hospitality, delicious dishes, and the perfect atmosphere.

Our Location

Kijiko Palace is located in the heart of the city at the corner of Main Street and River Road, just a short walk from the central market and the historic town square. Our convenient location makes us easy to reach by car, taxi, or public transport.

Whether you are arriving late after a long journey or stopping by in the early hours, Kijiko Palace is always open. Our doors are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so you can enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a midnight meal whenever you need it.

Why Choose Kijiko Palace?

At Kijiko Palace, we combine a welcoming atmosphere with around-the-clock service. Our chefs prepare fresh meals all day and night, and our friendly team is always available to help you choose the perfect dish.

Stop by for a comforting meal, celebrate with friends, or relax after a late shift. Kijiko Palace is your home for exceptional food and service anytime.
